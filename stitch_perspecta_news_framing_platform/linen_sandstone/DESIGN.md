## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a tactile, archival aesthetic reminiscent of heavy-stock literary broadsheets, bespoke curatorial monographs, and elite analytical journalism institutes. The personality is authoritative yet introspective, unhurried, and intellectually rigorous.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
- **Movement:** Tactile Editorial combined with warm Architectural Minimalism.
- **Atmosphere:** Evokes natural linen, aged deckle paper, sandstone architectural facades, and letterpress impressions. Interface elements avoid synthetic digital gloss, prioritizing subtle tonal shifts, delicate hairline rules, and typographic rhythm.
- **Emotional Impact:** Cultivates deep trust, deliberate reading, cognitive clarity, and aesthetic indulgence. The interface steps back to elevate synthesis, investigative nuance, and complex analytical discourse.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in unbleached organic fibers, mineral pigments, and archival inks. Every shade maintains warm, yellow-red undertones to prevent cold technical sterility.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas / Outer Parchment:** `#F7F3EE` — The primary foundation; an unbleached linen tone that acts as the physical floor for all views.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Section):** `#FAF7F2` — Soft bleached parchment for high-clarity content containers.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Muted Inset/Sub-card):** `#EFE8DF` — Natural sand tone for sidebars, active rows, and quiet groupings.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Recessed/Toolwells):** `#E6DDD2` — Compressed sandstone for inputs, chips, and navigational wells.

### Ink & Hierarchy
- **Primary Ink:** `#241E19` — Dark bistre/espresso. Rich and organic with near-black contrast without harsh synthetic black.
- **Secondary Ink:** `#5D544C` — Grounded taupe for deck copy, subheaders, and analytical summaries.
- **Muted Ink:** `#7C7167` — Weathered stone for metadata, datelines, timestamps, and secondary captions.
- **Hairlines & Dividers:** `#D8CFC4` (primary structure), `#E2D9CE` (interior sub-rules).

### Accents & Analytical Taxonomy
- **Brand Accent:** `#9E4A28` (Terracotta) / `#B85934` (Burnt Amber Hover) — Reserved for primary focal actions, active editorial tabs, and anchor callouts.
- **Analytical Tints (Subdued archival ink washes):**
  - **Facts (Sage/Moss):** `#3B6B56` (12% wash background: `#EBF1EE`)
  - **Opinions (Ochre/Amber):** `#A66B24` (12% wash background: `#FAF3EA`)
  - **Framing (Terracotta/Rust):** `#9E4A28` (12% wash background: `#F9EFEA`)
  - **Emotion (Plum/Wine):** `#7D374B` (12% wash background: `#F6ECF0`)
  - **Context / Omission (Slate/Indigo):** `#3C567A` (12% wash background: `#ECF1F6`)

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dual tension: the expansive, literary grace of **Newsreader** (used for headlines, thesis statements, editorial analysis, and longform body) balanced against the modern, clinical precision of **Hanken Grotesk** (applied to taxonomies, charts, analytical tags, and telemetry).

### Typographic Principles
- **Editorial Proportions:** Long-form bodies use an optical sizing feel with generous line heights (`body-lg` at `30px` on `19px` font) to replicate classic literary journals and reduce reading fatigue.
- **Casing & Micro-copy:** All `label-lg`, `label-md`, and `label-sm` tokens are deployed in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to balance the calligraphic warmth of Newsreader with scientific structure.
- **Editorial Emphasis:** Italics in Newsreader convey historical quotes, primary evidence excerpts, and original source citations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around an asymmetrical 12-column editorial grid inspired by archival broadsheets, scholarly reviews, and research briefs.

### Grid Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column dynamic grid, `3rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Allows classic 8/4 or 9/3 splits where primary analysis holds the broad column and empirical breakdowns / metadata occupy the narrower column. Max canvas bounded at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `2rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Secondary inspector columns tuck beneath primary dossiers or shift into stacked horizontal rails.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column linear grid, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Typography tightens to dedicated mobile levels; sidebars convert into sticky sheet anchors.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm relies heavily on structural hairline rules rather than empty digital volume.
- Component padding follows a compact, tactile rhythm (`space-md` for standard cells; `space-lg` to `space-xl` for editorial chapter transitions).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews buoyant, blur-heavy SaaS drop shadows in favor of a flat, layered paper and bookbinding hierarchy.

### Depth Mechanisms
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F7F3EE` acts as the archival base sheet.
- **Level 1 (Tonal Decking):** Elevated reading panels use `#FAF7F2` framed by subtle `1px` hairlines (`#D8CFC4`).
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays / Drawers):** Overlays, flyouts, and contextual dossiers sit atop the layout with an authentic low-diffusion ink veil shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(36, 30, 25, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(36, 30, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Deep Focus):** Full editorial focus states use a warm dimmed backdrop veil (`rgba(36, 30, 25, 0.45)`) combined with a defined container edge: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(36, 30, 25, 0.14)`.
- **Debossed Wells:** Filtering wells, metadata trays, and search fields drop down into the page using `#EFE8DF` or `#E6DDD2` with a subtle inner border, creating an engraved or blind-debossed bookbinding feel.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects crisp, Guillotine-cut paper edges and architectural stone forms. Roundedness is kept strictly to Level `1` (Soft: `0.25rem` / `4px`), ensuring components preserve an archival, print-first character.

### Shape Rules
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** `4px` corner radius. Crisp, tailored, and clean.
- **Analytical Chips & Badges:** `2px` or `4px` subtle radius; pill geometries are prohibited to preserve the editorial identity.
- **Article & Research Cards:** `4px` radius or strictly `0px` with hairline dividers for traditional print newspaper layouts.
- **Dividers:** Crisp `1px` solid hairlines, never dashed or blurred.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#9E4A28` (Terracotta), text `#FAF7F2`, `4px` border radius, uppercase `label-md` Hanken Grotesk with `0.06em` tracking. Padding: `10px 18px`. Hover: `#B85934`. Focus: 2px ring with `#9E4A28` offset by 2px in `#F7F3EE`.
- **Secondary / Archival Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #D8CFC4`, text `#241E19`. Hover: background `#EFE8DF`, border `#5D544C`.
- **Text Link / Dossier Link:** Newsreader italic or Hanken Grotesk caps with a persistent `1px` underline positioned 3px below the baseline, colored in `#9E4A28` with an opacity transition on hover.

### Chips & Analytical Tags
- **Base Geometry:** Height 24px, radius `3px`, uppercase `label-sm` font, `4px 8px` padding.
- **Taxonomy Variants:**
  - *Facts:* Background `#EBF1EE`, text `#3B6B56`, border `1px solid rgba(59, 107, 86, 0.25)`.
  - *Opinions:* Background `#FAF3EA`, text `#A66B24`, border `1px solid rgba(166, 107, 36, 0.25)`.
  - *Framing:* Background `#F9EFEA`, text `#9E4A28`, border `1px solid rgba(158, 74, 40, 0.25)`.
  - *Emotion:* Background `#F6ECF0`, text `#7D374B`, border `1px solid rgba(125, 55, 75, 0.25)`.
  - *Context/Omission:* Background `#ECF1F6`, text `#3C567A`, border `1px solid rgba(60, 86, 122, 0.25)`.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- **Editorial Card:** Surface `#FAF7F2`, border `1px solid #D8CFC4`, padding `space-lg`. Free of heavy drop shadows; cards differentiate via hairline structure and subtle background contrast against `#F7F3EE`.
- **Inspection Card:** Inset background `#EFE8DF`, border-left `3px solid #9E4A28` (or relevant analytical indicator tint), padding `space-md`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FAF7F2`, border `1px solid #D8CFC4`, radius `4px`, padding `10px 14px`. Typography: `body-md` in Newsreader for user narrative or `Hanken Grotesk` for structured parameters. Focus: border `#9E4A28`, subtle inset glow `0 0 0 1px #9E4A28`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom `16px` elements. Frame `1px solid #7C7167`, background `#FAF7F2`. Checked state fills `#241E19` with a `#FAF7F2` checkmark icon. Radios use `50%` roundness with a centered dot.

### Lists & Archival Index Tables
- Rows use `1px solid #E2D9CE` bottom dividers.
- Hovering an index entry shifts the row background to `#EFE8DF` with an immediate cursor cue. Column headers use `label-md` in `#7C7167` with subtle bottom border `#D8CFC4`.

### Specialized Perspective Components
- **Perspective Splitter / Claim Compare:** Side-by-side parallel columns rendered in `#FAF7F2` divided by a vertical `1px` rule (`#D8CFC4`), topped with an analytical categorization chip indicating ideological or methodological framing.
- **Source Ledger:** A compact tabular footer inside cards showing provenance, publisher rating, and citation integrity in `data-mono` type.
